# LanTal Mining Au (LMAU)

LMAu (LanTal Mining) is an stablecoin backed by physical gold reserves held in secure vaults. The custodian of the gold reserves is a specialized, licensed company with all the necessary equipment to verify the authenticity of the gold. LMAu tokens are issued in units of grams. Holders avoid the complexities of authenticating, transporting and costs of storage physical gold. You can read the roadmap and white paper [here](https://lmau.lantalmining.com/assets/LMAu-Whitepaper.pdf). Security audit is [here](https://github.com/CashivaCommunity/audits/blob/master/CashivaAuditReportForLanTalMining.pdf)
